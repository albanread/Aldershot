# Territory Manager and UK territory

Two native C modules: the Territory Manager (`territorymanager.c`, version 0.58, as RISC OS 5.30 runs) and the UK territory (`uk.c`, `Internat/Territory/TerritoryModule` 0.64). The manager keeps the list of registered territories and the configured one, and forwards the territory SWIs to it. The UK territory supplies character tables, collation, date and time formatting, and time zones. `timecalc.c` holds the time arithmetic.

The UK tables are generated byte for byte from the ROOL sources by `tools/mkterritory.py` into `territory_uk.c`. Texts come from `TerritoryManager:Messages` and `UK:Messages`.

## Territory Manager

SWI chunk &43040 (`api/defs/territory.toml` declares them all, typed).

| SWI | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Number, Select, Exists | The configured territory, select one, test if registered. |
| Register, Deregister | A territory registers a table of 26 entry points (for SWIs &4304A to &43063) and its private word. |
| NumberToName, AlphabetNumberToName, SelectAlphabet | Names, and the alphabet announcement (Service_International). |
| SetTime, ReadCurrentTimeZone, ConvertTimeToUTCOrdinals | Clock and zone. |
| DaylightSaving (&43077) | Reads or sets the DST bits, and tests or bounds DST for a zone. |
| ConvertTimeFormats (&43078) | Converts between local and UTC, in ordinals or five-byte form. |
| &4304A to &43063 | Forwarded to the territory. R0 = -1 means the configured territory. |

An unregistered territory gives &192 "Territory not present". Other errors are &190, &191, &195, &196, &197, and &1E8.

Commands: `*Territory [<name or number>]`, `*Territories`, and the `*Configure` keywords Territory, TimeZone, DST, NoDST, AutoDST and a zone name. `*Status` prints the three settings. The CMOS holds the territory (&18 EOR 1), the zone offset in quarter hours (&8B) and the DST bits (&DC bit 7, &10 bit 0).

With AutoDST set, the manager books an OS_CallAfter for the next DST change and issues Service_TimeZoneChanged (&81080) when the offset changes. It issues Service_TerritoryManagerLoaded (&64) and Service_TerritoryStarted (&75). At Service_PostInit, if the configured territory is not registered, it becomes the UK.

## UK territory

Territory 1, alphabet 101 (Latin1), no SWIs of its own. It registers 26 native entry points: time zone names (GMT and BST), date and time conversion with the RISC OS % fields, Collate, TransformString, the character tables and properties, ReadSymbols, ReadCalendarInformation, NameToNumber, and DaylightRules. Table addresses stay fixed for the life of the image, because clients such as the Filer keep them.

## Clients

The kernel's OS_ConvertDateAndTime and OS_ConvertStandardDateAndTime call the configured territory. The Filer, Wimp, Pinboard and BASIC use Collate, WriteDirection, LowerCaseTable and ReadCalendarInformation.

## Differences from RISC OS 5.30

- Help strings end "ROSGD native".
- Service_PostInit does not load a territory module from disc. It uses the UK.
- Service_UKWord is not taken. The clock is the host's, and OS_Word 15 sets nothing.
- ConvertDateAndTime with reserved flag bits corrupts the stack in the original. Here it returns "Unknown '%' field" (&2C2).
- Collate does not loop for ever on equal strings when R3's top bits are set.
- ReadSymbols past item 18 returns 0.
- `*Configure` and `*Status` are the BOX's (`runtime/oscli.c`). They do not yet cover the kernel's own options.
- OS_Word 14 and 15, `Sys$Time`, `Sys$Date`, `Sys$Year` and `*Time` are the runtime's own and use UTC or the host's time.

Tests: `tests/desktop/territory` (`compare.py --farm`, with `cases`) compares with RISC OS 5.30 on the farm. `boot/selftest_territory.c` checks the rest.

Sources followed: the 5.30 Territory Manager 0.58 (read from the ROM, as its source is not in the tree), and `Internat/Territory/TerritoryManager` 0.59 and `TerritoryModule` 0.64 on ROOL's GitLab.
