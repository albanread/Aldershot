# Studio

!Studio is a multitrack studio. WAV, AIFF and FLAC sounds dropped on it become clips on tracks, drawn with their waveforms along a timeline. Clips are moved, put on another track, cut in two at the playhead and thrown away, and the whole arrangement is mixed down to a WAV, AIFF or FLAC file. It saves the arrangement as a session (type &A7D). Its engine is built on SoundCraft.

`Studio.mojo` is the application. It needs the shared shell, `../shell/rosapp.mojo`.
