# Micropolis

!Micropolis is the city simulation. Its engine runs Micropolis, Electronic Arts' 2008 GPL release of the original city simulation, on the box's Linux side; the graphics card draws the city where the box's presenter can. The tool palette lays roads, rails and power lines, zones the land and builds; the properties pane has the city's figures, the budget, the evaluation, graphs and the map. The City menu starts a new city, and the Scenarios menu loads one of the eight scenarios.

`Micropolis.mojo` is the front end. It needs the shared shell, `../shell/rosapp.mojo`. The engine, under the GPL, is published with the simulation in [box-micropolis](https://github.com/albanread/box-micropolis).
