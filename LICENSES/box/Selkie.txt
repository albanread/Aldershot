selkie-rs
=========

Version:  0.3.0
From:     https://github.com/btucker/selkie
Used by:  !Draft's diagram engine (ports/rosapps/diagram-engine): Mermaid's
          own languages -- flowchart, sequence, class, entity relationship,
          state, gantt, git, journey, timeline, C4 and architecture -- and
          the dagre layout that places a flowchart's nodes
Licence:  MIT

selkie's source is kept beside the engine unchanged, except that its
command-line programs and its PNG and SVG writers are left out: BOX parses
and lays out with selkie and then draws the result itself, so that a diagram
in a document is drawn the way the rest of the document is.
